# Accessible websites: first-pass checklist

- Semantic landmarks and hierarchical headings.
- Keyboard-only navigation, visible focus and skip links.
- Form labels, help text and understandable errors.
- Alternatives for informative images and media.
- Adequate text contrast and touch target sizes.
- Responsive zoom and reflow.
- Dialog focus management and reduced-motion preferences.
- Manual testing plus automated audits: neither replaces the other.
